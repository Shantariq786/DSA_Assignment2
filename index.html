Data and methods

Publications missing year, author or any author affiliation are excluded. Author IDs identify nodes; repeated collaborations are combined into one weighted link. Primary country is the most frequent recognized country per author, with alphabetical tie-breaking. Unknown countries and countries outside the top ten are gray (#A9A9A9).

Hover matches any complete affiliation shared by two authors, normalized for case and whitespace. Different department names or address spellings are not automatically merged. All affiliations are retained in the author details.
